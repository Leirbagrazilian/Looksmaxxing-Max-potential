How this works

478 landmarks are measured on your photo and compared with the same measurements on {{ "{:,}".format(a.n_reference) }} faces rated by 60 people each (SCUT-FBP5500). Head angle, expression and sharpness are removed statistically. A frontal photo can't measure profile features (maxilla, chin projection), hair, height, body or style. Most raters were young Chinese adults.
